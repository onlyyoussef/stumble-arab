## 🎮 Usage

### Step 4: Maps & Emotes
1. **Select Maps**: Click on map cards to select (green border indicates selection)
2. **Filter by Type**: Use tabs to filter by Elimination, Race, Shooter, Driving, or Collect
3. **Search**: Type in search box to find specific maps
4. **Configure Emotes**: Switch to Emotes tab
5. **Disable Emotes**: Click emotes to disable them (all others will be allowed)
6. **Filter by Category**: Use tabs to filter by Basic, Dance, Action, Seasonal, Premium, or Special
7. **Sync to All Rounds**: Use "SYNC TO ALL" button to apply settings to all rounds
